```
T_core > 15 × 10⁶ K

```

- M(r) = Mass enclosed within radius r

## Carbon Formation

### Triple-Alpha Process

Carbon forms via **helium burning** in red giants:

```
⁴He + ⁴He ⇌ ⁸Be  (unstable, τ ≈ 10⁻¹⁶ s)
⁸Be + ⁴He → ¹²C + γ  (resonance at 7.65 MeV)
```

**Hoyle Resonance:**
- Fine-tuned energy level in ¹²C
- Without it, no significant carbon production
- "Anthropic coincidence" or design?

**SSZ Perspective:**
- Segment structure may **favor** resonant states
- Energy levels emerge from geometric quantization
- Not coincidence, but geometry

### Oxygen Formation

Once carbon exists:

```
¹²C + ⁴He → ¹⁶O + γ
```

But:
- Reaction rate must be **tuned** to produce both C and O
- Too fast → all C converts to O (no organic chemistry)
- Too slow → no O (no water, no respiration)

**Observed:** Reaction rates are "just right" (another coincidence?)

**SSZ:** Segment-mediated cross-sections naturally balance rates.

## Supernovae & Element Distribution

### Core Collapse (Type II SNe)

Massive stars (M > 8 M☉) end in supernova:

1. Core fusion produces Fe (most stable nucleus)
2. No further fusion possible (endothermic)
3. Core collapses → neutron star or black hole
4. Shock wave ejects outer layers
5. **Elements scattered into interstellar medium**

### r-Process (Rapid Neutron Capture)

Heavy elements (Au, Pt, U) form via:

```
Seed nucleus + rapid neutron capture → heavy isotopes → β-decay
```

**Requirements:**
- Extreme neutron flux (n > 10²⁴ cm⁻³)
- High temperature (T > 10⁹ K)
- Short timescale (τ ≈ 1 s)

**Sites:**
- Core-collapse supernovae
- Neutron star mergers (confirmed by GW170817)
